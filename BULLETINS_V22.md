# V22 — Official mountain & avalanche bulletins

V22 introduces a source-first bulletin panel.

## Sources
- AINEVA official avalanche bulletins: https://aineva.it/bollettini/
- EAWS standards and European avalanche warning services: https://www.avalanches.org/

## Design rule
The app does not derive an avalanche danger level from weather-model variables. Official bulletins remain authoritative; the app may display their metadata and link to the original publication.
